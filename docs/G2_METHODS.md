# G2 methods and limits - 7 October 2026

The reviewed G1 vintage is held fixed. No G2 downloads, interpolation, trimming, seasonal re-adjustment, outlier removal, paid API calls or proxy-SVAR are performed. Code, configuration, input hashes and library versions identify the run. `configs/g2.yaml` fixes seed 20261007, horizons 0-36, 499 residual-block bootstrap replications and 1,000 accepted sign directions. Results are awaiting G2 review; G3 drafting, theory notes and publication remain outside this gate.

## Samples and transformations

EA baseline: September 2004-July 2026, ECB two-year yield; 263 months. EA activity/prices/FX/Brent are 100 log levels, VIX unscaled log level; rates are percentage-point levels. TR AOFM baseline: January 2011-July 2026, 187 months. The announced-rate robustness uses January 2006-July 2026, O/N borrowing then one-week repo, with G1 calendar-day weighting. All samples and variants are in the configuration. No TR bond-yield substitution.

TR levels is fitted first. BIC selects lag 2, companion radius 1.012981: the approved instability rule triggers monthly inflation (100 times log CPI difference). The full levels fit/diagnostics remain in the transform decision and a separate levels-comparison run. The inflation baseline remains unstable (radius 1.012320); no additional transforms or lag changes are made to force stability. EA retains approved levels despite radius 1.002906. Finite-horizon calculations exist for unstable models, but conventional stationary long-run interpretations and nominal confidence coverage are unreliable. Root flags and counts of unstable bootstrap draws are retained, not discarded.

ADF/PP/KPSS (constant and trend specifications) plus Zivot-Andrews are diagnostics. CPI level/difference results are provided for both 2006-2026 and the 2011 baseline; FX/REER use the longer potential VECM window. The transparent I(1) agreement screen requires trend-level ADF/PP nonrejection, trend-KPSS rejection, differenced constant-ADF/PP rejection and constant-KPSS nonrejection at 5%. I(2)-compatible requires differenced ADF/PP nonrejection and KPSS rejection. Mixed tests establish neither order. The agreement screen is deliberately conservative and not proof of integration order. Johansen/VECM is not estimated when any variable fails this screen; observed CPI/FX failures are not relaxed.

## Reduced-form VAR and recursive identification

Equationwise OLS with constant and eleven month dummies. Already adjusted IP is not re-adjusted. Monthly price seasonality is represented deterministically; the same deterministic terms occur in every equation. Overnight models have a documented October 2019 transition level dummy, with raw G1 EONIA/ESTR splice retained. The EA sample ending December 2019 is labelled pre-ZLB-heavy and not ZLB-free. AIC/BIC/HQ use a common candidate-lag estimation window, free regression-parameter counts and log determinant of the common-divisor residual covariance (u'u/n). BIC selects among feasible lags 1-12; selected lag is refitted on the complete requested sample. These are Gaussian conditional-OLS information criteria, not a SUR refit. Residual degrees of freedom must be at least ten. Earlier TR regime fits cap lags at two, fixed before seeing their results.

EA order: Brent, VIX, activity, price, policy, EUR/USD. TR: Brent, VIX, Fed funds, activity, price, policy, USD/TRY. TR external equations omit all domestic lags; lower-triangular Cholesky implies zero domestic-shock impact on external variables and zero subsequent external responses. This is an imposed small-open-economy assumption. The Cholesky policy column is divided by its contemporaneous rate response: one rate unit = one percentage point = 100 bp. No simultaneous activity/price response is allowed under recursive ordering. EUR/USD increases imply EUR appreciation; USD/TRY increases imply TRY depreciation.

Stability uses companion eigenvalue moduli (<1), not statsmodels' inverse-root convention. Equation-specific Breusch-Godfrey LM diagnostics at 1/6/12 residual lags include the original regressors and lagged vectors of all residuals; insufficient rank/df is reported as unavailable. Jarque-Bera results are marginal, not a multivariate normality test. Conditional policy-lag Wald tests are descriptive Granger diagnostics, not causal identification. Multiple diagnostic p-values are not adjusted and must not be read as a specification search.

## Uncertainty, local projections and signs

Circular moving-block resampling of centered residual vectors, block six months, conditional on observed initial lags. Every draw refits the selected specification, recomputes the Cholesky shock and estimates both VAR and LP responses. Fixed lag, transforms and model selection; no unconditional selection-adjusted inference. Checkpoints are atomic and resume from independent draw seeds tied to model name and the common seed. Finite unstable draws are retained and counted. Nonfinite/failed calculations are counted and bounded, never silently replaced indefinitely. Bands are pointwise percentile 68/90%, not simultaneous bands; their nominal coverage is particularly uncertain for these unstable models.

Jorda LP uses the same identified recursive rate-unit shocks, original lag/deterministic controls and current earlier-ordered variables. FWL estimates exactly match OLS in tests. Native HAC standard errors use Bartlett Newey-West lag h+1 and residual-df correction; they are provided separately. Joint bootstrap bands propagate shock-estimation uncertainty. The resampled histories are generated by the fitted VAR: LP uncertainty therefore also conditions on that VAR data-generating approximation, which is consequential when observed LP paths differ from VAR predictions. TR inflation and growth robustness are accumulated within each bootstrap draw to compare price/activity/FX **levels**; native inflation/HAC quantities remain separate. HAC standard errors are never summed across horizons. External LP outcomes are set to zero by the same TR block assumption.

Sign restrictions draw one Haar-marginal orthogonal direction at a fixed reduced form, restricted to the domestic subspace for TR. Positive policy and nonpositive price-level response at horizons 0-3, no activity/FX restriction. For inflation models, the price restriction applies to cumulative responses. Orientation is flipped to positive rate impact; a declared 0.1 residual-standard-deviation impact floor avoids unbounded 100-bp normalization. Accepted/attempted/weak-direction counts are reported. Accepted-direction percentile spread is identification uncertainty, **not confidence intervals**; componentwise median paths need not represent one admissible model. Comparison to a recursive price puzzle is partly mechanical because signs impose the initial price direction.

## FEVD, historical accounting and regimes

Recursive FEVD uses unnormalized mutually orthogonal shocks, forecast horizons 1-36 and all shock columns; point shares sum to one. FEVD is for native modeled variables: EA log prices, TR monthly inflation. It is not a TR accumulated-price FEVD. Historical decomposition retains actual initial lags, deterministic terms and every recursive shock; exact reconstruction is checked. EA log-price components are differenced to monthly inflation; TR inflation components already have that unit. Bootstrap HD parameters are fitted on resampled histories, then applied to the **actual** history to show conditional parameter uncertainty. Private accounting tables include every shock, not just the policy contribution.

TR regimes: January 2011-May 2018 (89 months), June 2018-May 2023 (60), June 2023-July 2026 (38). The final regime has **no VAR**: four domestic-variable LP, one domestic lag, monthly dummies and contemporaneous earlier variables. It uses the retrospective full-sample AOFM recursive shock; it does not independently identify recent-regime innovations. The recent LP divides every response by its own horizon-zero policy coefficient (0.5313 in the unscaled point fit), repeated in each bootstrap draw, so the shown recent tightening has a 100-bp impact. HAC SEs condition on the point-estimated divisor; joint bootstrap propagates its uncertainty and re-estimates the full-sample shock. A nonfinite or near-zero divisor rejects a draw with a counted failure. Horizons leaving fewer than ten residual degrees of freedom are missing and never extrapolated.

All robustness changes: announced TR indicator/longer window; EA raw overnight on common/long windows; German zero-coupon two-year yield/longer window (Source: Deutsche Bundesbank; author monthly aggregation); headline-to-core replacements with complete current-base histories; growth of log variables with **rates kept in levels**; preserved TR levels; two earlier regime VARs and recent LP. Keeping rates in levels in G2 growth robustness differs explicitly from G1's generic all-column difference QA file. This change is fixed in code/configuration, not chosen to eliminate a puzzle. No core splice or bridge-threshold change.

## Outputs and reproduction

`make g2-pilot` runs twenty replications per baseline; `make g2` computes full offline estimates from local licensed G1 inputs, with resumable private caches. `make figures` creates public author-estimate plots/tables and private source-derived review checks; `make g2-summary` renders the one-page PDF; `make audit-g2` checks lineage/accounting/privacy read-only. `make quick` exercises G1 and all G2 models on temporary seeded synthetic data with no credentials/network and without overwriting empirical artifacts. Public results are authored model coefficients/responses/aggregate diagnostics; source observations, innovations, historical accounting and rebasing checks stay under ignored `data/` or `runs/`. Frozen vintage source files require local/private access; no claim of unrestricted raw-data portability. Estimates and synthetic tests are separate.

Verified primary references: [Sims, Stock and Watson (1990)](https://doi.org/10.2307/2938337), [Jorda (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1257/0002828053828518), [Rubio-Ramirez, Waggoner and Zha (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-937X.2009.00578.x). Implementation cross-checks use [statsmodels VAR](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/vector_ar.html); primary discussion of sign-set inference: [Moon and Schorfheide working paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w17140). These explain methods, not the validity of the chosen monetary-policy indicators.
